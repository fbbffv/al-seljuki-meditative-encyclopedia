Between the Blazing Fire and the Tranquility of Safety

When masks fall… and nothing remains except what the heart and man have put forward

✍ Ali Khalil Abbas Al-Seljuki Al-Turkmani Al-Lailani Al-Seljuki Meditative Encyclopedia
"Is he who is cast into the Fire better or he who comes secure on the Day of Resurrection? Do what you will; indeed, He is Seeing of what you do."
[Fussilat: 40]

✍️ There are verses that merely pass by the ear, and there are verses that stop the heart between the ribs, suddenly placing man before himself, stripping him of all the excuses and illusions he hides behind.

And this verse is one of those that leave no escape for the heedless, no excuse for the hesitant, and no hiding place for the arrogant.

"Is he who is cast into the Fire better or he who comes secure on the Day of Resurrection?"

Which of the two do you want for yourself?

Do you want a fate in which man is cast headlong into the Fire, after he used to cast Allah’s commands and prohibitions behind his back in the world?

Or do you want to come secure on the Day of Resurrection, when hearts tremble, eyes are dazed, paths narrow, and every person searches for a foothold to be saved?

What a word!

Secure.

He did not merely say: comes on the Day of Resurrection saved, but rather used the expression of security (safety); because the Day of Resurrection is a day of panic, a day of upheaval, a day when scales shift, truths are laid bare, and man learns that the greatest thing he needed all his life was what he sometimes thought was far off: Allah’s mercy and His security.

The Beginning of the Path: When the Heart Deviates from Truth

Allah Almighty said:

"Indeed, those who deviate concerning Our verses are not hidden from Us."

Deviating concerning the verses means veering away from the truth, distorting their meanings, and dealing with them through desire, stubbornness, and disrespect.

A person may deceive people, argue with them, beautify falsehood with an eloquent tongue, dress doubt in the garments of knowledge, and raise the slogan of freedom while being a captive to his desire, or raise the slogan of intellect while rejecting the truth because it does not suit his wishes...

But:

"are not hidden from Us."

What you hid is exposed.

What you planned is known.

What your tongue uttered is preserved.

What settled in your heart is known.

And what you did in the dark is present in Allah’s knowledge.

So do not be deceived by your ability to escape the eyes of humans.

For you can hide from people, but you cannot hide from Allah.

You can lock the door of your house, but you cannot lock the doors of the heavens against your deeds.

You can delete a message from your phone, but you cannot erase it from Allah’s knowledge.

You can deny a word you spoke, but Allah knows what was said and what lay behind the speech.

You can conceal your intention from the closest people to you, but it is not hidden from Him who knows the treachery of the eyes and what the breasts conceal.

The World Is a Short Stage… and the Hereafter Is the Moment Truth Is Unveiled

How many a person in the world appears mighty, while with Allah he is humiliated.

And how many a person appears weak in the eyes of people, while with Allah he is grand.

And how many a person fills the screens with his picture, whose name buzzes in gatherings, for whom doors are opened, and people stand in respect; yet with Allah he carries no weight.

And how many a man is known to no one, living far from the spotlight, possessing neither fame nor authority, yet when he is alone with his Lord he weeps, when he errs he seeks forgiveness, when given he thanks, when tested he is patient, and when capable of oppression he fears Allah...

That is the one who truly deserves to be said of him:

He knows the path of security.

For Allah does not look at the size of the image in people’s eyes, but rather at the reality of the heart and action.

The Security People Search for in the World

All people are searching for security.

• This one searches for job security.

• And this one searches for financial security.

• And this one searches for the security of home.

• And this one searches for the security of power.

• And this one searches for the security of relationships.

• And this one searches for the security of fame and acceptance.

• And this one searches for the security of health.

Yet no matter what means of the world a person possesses, his heart remains prone to anxiety.

Because the heart does not rest completely unless it knows its Lord.

A person may be in a spacious palace, yet his heart is constricted.

And he may be in a small house, yet his heart is filled with tranquility.

The owner of millions may sleep on a luxurious bed, yet wake up terrified of the future.

And a poor person possessing but a little may sleep, placing his head on his pillow saying:

Praise be to Allah, my Lord is with me.

Here appears the difference between possessing the means of security and possessing security itself.

The Greatest Security Is for Your Heart to Be with Allah

True security is not that you guarantee the world will not hurt you.

For the world is an abode of trial.

You will fall ill.

You will lose.

You will grieve.

You will disagree with people.

Heavy days will pass over you.

And news you never expected will come to you.

Yet the believer possesses something far greater than a guarantee of no pain:

He possesses the certainty that pain is not in vain, and that Allah does not neglect His servant.

When blessings touch him, he says: Praise be to Allah.

And when calamity strikes him, he says: Allah has decreed, and whatever He willed has happened.

And when he sins, he does not flee from Allah, but flees to Allah.

And when the world narrows around him, he raises his hands to the heavens.

And when the roads grow dark, his heart remains attached to the light of Allah.

And this is the security that banks cannot build, walls cannot guard, and positions cannot grant.

"Do what you will; indeed, He is Seeing of what you do."

"Do what you will"… What a word to shake the heart!

Then comes the conclusion:

"Do what you will; indeed, He is Seeing of what you do."

What a phrase that, if it entered the heart as it ought to, would transform a person’s life!

It is not a permission for recklessness.

Nor an authorization for disobedience.

Rather, it is a warning to awaken the heedless:

Do what you will, but remember that you are under the eye of Allah.

Say what you will, and Allah hears.

Do what you will, and Allah sees.

Choose what you will, then await the outcome of your choice.

Nothing is absent from Him.

Not the movement of a hand.

Nor the step of a foot.

Nor the beat of a heart.

Nor the whisper of a tongue.

Nor the privacy of a secret.

Nor the tear of the oppressed.

Nor the sigh of the brokenhearted.

Nor the smile of the righteous.

Nor the charity of the poor.

Nor the prostration of the worshiper.

Nor the seeking of forgiveness by the repentant.

Everything with Him is known.

O Son of Adam… You Are But Days

Man proceeds in the world as though he were abiding in it forever.

He postpones repentance.

And postpones prayer.

And postpones returning rights.

And postpones apologizing.

And postpones rectifying the heart.

And says:

I will change tomorrow.

Yet tomorrow is not his to own.

How many a person slept with long plans in his heart, then never woke up.

And how many a person left his home and never returned.

And how many a person said: I will visit so-and-so tomorrow, only for tomorrow to find him in another world.

And how many a person planned for ten years ahead, while not owning even a single hour after that moment.

Therefore, do not delay returning to Allah.

For you do not need a new appointment to repent.

Nor do you need to wait for your life to become ideal.

Start from where you are.

Even if by a single step.

Even if by a supplication.

Even if by two units of prayer.

Even if by a tear.

Even if by the word:

O Lord, I have erred… so forgive me.

How beautiful is a servant knocking at Allah’s door after a long absence!

For indeed, Allah never tires of accepting the repentant.

Do Not Be Deceived by Allah’s Concealment

Among the most dangerous things a person can be deceived by is that Allah concealed him.

He sins and is not exposed.

Then he sins again and is not exposed.

So he thinks the matter has ended.

Unaware that concealment is a blessing and an opportunity, not a certificate of innocence.

If Allah conceals you, then feel shame before the One who covered you.

If you err in seclusion, do not say: No one saw me.

Rather say:

Allah saw me.

And if you intend oppression, remember:

Allah saw me.

And if you intend betrayal of trust:

Allah saw me.

And if you intend to say a word that hurts a person:

Allah saw me.

This word alone, if it settled in the heart, would become a guardian that never slumbers.

Allah saw me.

How grand it is in secret!

And how grand it is when a person is capable of sin, yet leaves it out of fear of Allah.

Faith Is Not Merely Fearing the Fire

Faith is not mere fear.

It is a fear that drives to action, a hope that drives to obedience, and a love that makes the heart long for its Lord.

You fear Him because you know His Majesty.

And hope in Him because you know His Mercy.

And love Him because you know His Blessings.

And obey Him because you know your entire life stands upon His grace.

When awe, love, and hope gather in the heart, the heart becomes alive.

As for the heart that does not fear, it may feel secure from Allah’s plan.

And the heart that does not hope may despair.

And the heart that does not love turns worship into a cold habit.

So let your heart be between fear and hope, walking to Allah on two wings.

The Day All Names Fall Away

A day will come when the family name will not avail.

Nor the position.

Nor the wealth.

Nor the fame.

Nor the abundance of followers.

Nor the power of tribe.

Nor human influence.

Nor one's image before people.

There, nothing remains except:

What were you? And what did you do?

There, every title falls away except the title of servitude.

And every authority ends except the authority of Allah.

And man comes to know that the greatest thing he possessed in the world was not wealth nor status, but the opportunity in which he could return to Allah.

Blessed is he who recognized the value of the opportunity before it ended.

Build Your Security from Now

If you want to come on the Day of Resurrection secure, then start today.

• Pray before you lose the ability to pray.

• Read the Quran before the copy in your home becomes a witness to your abandonment of it.

• Wash your heart with repentance before human hands wash you.

• Forgive before a day comes when you wish you had forgiven.

• Return rights before you stand before their owner on a day when apologies are of no avail.

• Guard your tongue before words turn into burdens on your scale.

• Lower your gaze before looking becomes an arrow in your heart.

• Reconcile what is between you and people as much as you can.

• And multiply seeking forgiveness.

• And think well of Allah along with doing good deeds.

For You Are Choosing Your Scene There Right Now

Imagine yourself on the Day of Resurrection...

No wealth.

No phone.

No position.

No home.

No guards.

No crowd.

No one capable of carrying anything on your behalf.

Then it is said to you: This is what your hands have sent forth.

So tell me...

Do you want to come secure?

Then build that security from now.

Do not wait until you reach the Hereafter to search for it.

For security there is the fruit of faith here, the fruit of repentance here, the fruit of truthfulness here, and the fruit of reverence here.

There the fruit is harvested, and here the seed is planted.

Conclusion: Choose Your Path Before the Path Ends

O Son of Adam...

The road is before you, the hour passes, the pages are folded, life decreases, and death asks no permission.

So do not let your concern be that people know who you are, but let your concern be that Allah knows from you what He loves.

Do not make your greatest success gathering the world, but coming out of it with a heart you have not corrupted.

Do not make your biggest fear losing something of the world, but losing the pleasure of Allah.

And do not make your final wish to live longer, but to meet Allah while He is pleased with you.

For there...

When people are in turmoil, you will want security.

And when people panic, you will want tranquility.

And when people regret, you will want salvation.

And when people wish to return, you will want to have chosen well.

That is why the Quran places the two paths before your eyes:

"Is he who is cast into the Fire better"

"or he who comes secure on the Day of Resurrection?"

Then it leaves you before your responsibility:

"Do what you will; indeed, He is Seeing of what you do."

So choose for yourself before a day comes when choice is no longer in your hands.

Choose light before darkness.

Choose obedience before heartbreak.

Choose repentance before the death rattle.

Choose the Quran before loss.

Choose pardon before dispute.

Choose justice before the reckoning.

Choose Allah before searching for Allah in an hour when regret is of no avail.

For between you and that Day are but numbered days.

And between you and the grave is but a step you do not know when it will be.

And between you and security is only that you be truthful with Allah.

O Allah, do not make us among those who are cast into the Fire, and make us among those who come to You on the Day of Resurrection secure and reassured.

O Allah, we are weak, so strengthen us; we are sinful, so forgive us; we are failing, so assist us in Your obedience; and we are returning to You, so do not turn us back disappointed.

O Allah, make the last of our speech in this world the testimony that there is no god but Allah, and make the best of our days the day we meet You, and make us among those to whom Your angels say: "Do not fear and do not grieve but receive good tidings of Paradise, which you were promised."

O Allah, we ask for security on the Day of the Greatest Terror, light on the day faces darken, firmness on the day feet slip, and mercy on the day when nothing avails except Your mercy.
